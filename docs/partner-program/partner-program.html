Rethrow Partner Program

Every builder gets options. Every partner gets a fair shot.

Some Rethrow customers will want more than software: a hand-held build, a redesign, an integration, a security review. Today those requests would go to whichever partner we happen to know best. The Partner Program replaces that with structure: one portal where agencies and freelancers apply, get trained, and compete openly for work, and where Rethrow earns a fee for every deal it sources.

1Three pillars

Portal

Join and stay current

  • Apply to the program
  • Register deals and clients
  • Maintain a partner profile: skills, industries, rates, case studies
Training

Rethrow certified

  • Video courses on Rethrow and getting the most from Studio
  • Completing training unlocks a Certified badge
  • Certification is the only gate to the bid board
Referrals

Open bid board

  • Customer needing help posts an RFP in the portal
  • Certified partners bid
  • Customer picks the partner they like most, all in the portal

Why structure matters: the same RFP reaches every certified partner, the customer makes the choice, and the rules are published. No partner can say another one got preferential treatment, and customers can solicit multiple bids instead of taking our word for it.

3Referral fee and forecast

Proposed fee: 5% of deal value under $100k, 1% for deals of $100k or more. Assumes a $25k average deal.

Where I'd value your thoughts

  1. Fee cliff: flat 5% / 1% by deal size, or marginal tiers?
  2. Scope: fee on Rethrow-sourced RFPs only, or on every partner deal, including clients partners bring themselves?
  3. Who pays: deduct from the partner's award, or add to the customer's invoice?
  4. Leakage: run payments through the portal (for example Stripe Connect) so deals don't move off-platform?
  5. Quality bar: ratings, minimum delivered projects, or certification alone?
  6. Timing: launch with GA in January, or wait for enough customer demand to keep partners engaged?
